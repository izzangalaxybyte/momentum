# Momentum

A personal fitness and weight-loss coach for Android.

- Tells you what to do today based on what you did before: walking/running distance, strength
  weights and reps, with progressive overload that works with limited home equipment.
- Food tracker with a built-in Pakistani/desi food database, measured in roti, plates, bowls and cups.
- Reads workouts, steps, heart rate and weight from a Samsung Galaxy Watch via Health Connect.
- Exercise demos and form cues for every exercise.
- AI coach (Claude Haiku via OpenRouter) for weekly reviews and logging meals from text or photos.
- Offline-first app with a small serverless AWS backend (Lambda + DynamoDB).

Status: planning. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
